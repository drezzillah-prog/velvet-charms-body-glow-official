# Velvet Charms Body Glow — prelaunch legal & cosmetics gate

This file is an internal launch checklist. It is not customer-facing copy and must not be treated as legal advice.

## Seller information required before production launch

Do not publish a final Terms / Trader Information page until the real seller details are supplied and verified. Required fields include the trader's legal name, geographic address, direct contact email, trade-register/company registration details where applicable, and VAT details where applicable. These details must be available to the customer before the purchase is concluded.

## Returns and cancellations

- Standard distance-sale goods generally carry the EU 14-day withdrawal right.
- Goods made to the customer's specifications or clearly personalised can fall within the personalised-goods exception in Article 16(c) of Directive 2011/83/EU.
- Sealed goods unsuitable for return for health-protection or hygiene reasons may have a separate exception after unsealing where the legal conditions are actually met; do not apply this automatically to every cosmetic/body-care product.
- The personalised/hygiene exceptions must not be presented as removing statutory remedies for faulty, damaged, misdescribed or non-conforming goods.
- The customer must be told in advance who bears return costs where the ordinary withdrawal right applies.
- The site's final policy must distinguish ordinary catalogue goods, made-to-order goods that are not actually personalised, clearly personalised goods, any genuinely applicable opened-hygiene exception, and faulty/non-conforming goods.

## Shipping

Current site behavior intentionally treats the displayed checkout amount as the product total only. The cart must state clearly that shipping is additional, calculated from destination + parcel size + weight, and that no shipping charge is taken without the customer's approval. The final shipping policy should describe the calculation/approval step without promising fixed transit times that have not been confirmed. Do not display a free-shipping threshold unless a real threshold is later adopted.

## Reference photographs / personal data

- Reference photos are private and are used only to assess/create the requested commission.
- Public use, portfolio use, social-media use or reuse for marketing must require separate permission; purchase itself is not marketing consent.
- GDPR principles require purpose limitation, data minimisation, storage limitation and appropriate confidentiality/security measures.
- Private access links expire; the final Privacy Policy must describe the actual operational retention/deletion process accurately rather than promising a period that operations do not follow.
- Final Privacy Policy needs the real controller identity/contact details, legal basis/purposes, retention logic, data-subject rights and the actual processors in use (for example Vercel, Formspree and PayPal as applicable).

## Cosmetic products — hard launch gate

No cosmetic SKU should be placed on the EU market merely because the website checkout works. Before sale, each cosmetic product needs the compliance work applicable under Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009, including:

- an EU Responsible Person;
- manufacture consistent with cosmetic Good Manufacturing Practice;
- a cosmetic product safety assessment and Cosmetic Product Safety Report (CPSR) prepared by a suitably qualified safety assessor before the product is placed on the market;
- a Product Information File (PIF), kept for 10 years after the last batch was placed on the market;
- CPNP notification before the product is placed on the EU market;
- compliant formulation and ingredient restrictions;
- final physical label information under Article 19, including the Responsible Person details, nominal content where required, durability/PAO information where applicable, precautions, batch/reference identification, product function where not obvious, and INCI ingredient list;
- evidence supporting any explicit or implicit cosmetic performance claim.

The catalogue currently does not contain verified final INCI/label/CPSR/CPNP data. Do not invent these values in website copy. Add them only from the final verified product dossiers/labels.

## Cosmetic claims

Keep website claims cosmetic, clear and evidence-based. Article 20 of Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009 prohibits presenting a cosmetic as having characteristics/functions it does not have. Avoid medical or therapeutic claims unless the product is lawfully classified and supported for that purpose. Ingredient properties must not be presented as proven finished-product effects without adequate evidence.

## Non-cosmetic Body Glow products — GPSR review

Candles, textiles, accessories and other non-food consumer goods may fall under Regulation (EU) 2023/988 (GPSR), applicable since 13 December 2024. Before applicable products are offered online, confirm the manufacturer/responsible economic operator, product identification/traceability, applicable warnings and safety information, risk/technical documentation and the distance-sale information required by Article 19 GPSR. Do not invent manufacturer addresses, product identifiers or safety warnings before the physical product/documentation is final.

## Official reference points used for this checklist

- Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009 on cosmetic products, especially Articles 8, 10, 11, 13, 19 and 20.
- European Commission Cosmetic Product Notification Portal (CPNP) guidance.
- Regulation (EU) 2023/988 on general product safety for applicable non-cosmetic goods, especially Article 19 for distance sales.
- EU Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU, especially Articles 9 and 16, and current Your Europe B2C distance-selling guidance.
- GDPR principles under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 for customer/reference photographs and other personal data.

## Launch decision

Technical readiness is not the same as regulatory readiness. Body Glow can move to production only after the seller-information fields are confirmed and each cosmetic SKU has the real compliance dossier/label/notification required before sale. Applicable non-cosmetic SKUs must also pass their product-safety/traceability review before being offered to EU consumers.
