# Velvet Charms Body Glow — prelaunch legal & cosmetics gate

This file is an internal launch checklist. It is not customer-facing copy and must not be treated as legal advice.

## Seller information required before production launch

Do not publish a final Terms / Trader Information page until the real seller details are supplied and verified. Required fields include the trader's legal name, geographic address, direct contact email, trade-register/company registration details where applicable, and VAT details where applicable. These details must be available to the customer before the purchase is concluded.

## Returns and cancellations

- Standard distance-sale goods generally carry the EU 14-day withdrawal right.
- Goods made to the customer's specifications or clearly personalised can fall within the personalised-goods exception.
- The exception must not be presented as removing statutory remedies for faulty, damaged, misdescribed or non-conforming goods.
- The customer must be told in advance who bears return costs where the ordinary withdrawal right applies.
- The site's final policy must distinguish ordinary catalogue goods, made-to-order goods that are not actually personalised, clearly personalised goods, opened hygiene-sensitive goods where the legal exception applies, and faulty/non-conforming goods.

## Shipping

Current site behavior intentionally treats the displayed checkout amount as the product total only. The cart must state clearly that shipping is additional, calculated from destination + parcel size + weight, and that no shipping charge is taken without the customer's approval. The final shipping policy should describe the calculation method and approval step without promising fixed transit times that have not been confirmed.

## Reference photographs / personal data

- Reference photos are private and are used only to assess/create the requested commission.
- Public use, portfolio use, social-media use or reuse for marketing must require separate permission; purchase itself is not marketing consent.
- Current private links expire after 30 days. Operational retention/deletion rules still need to be stated accurately in the final Privacy Policy.
- Final Privacy Policy needs the real controller identity/contact details and the actual processors in use (for example Vercel, Formspree and PayPal as applicable).

## Cosmetic products — hard launch gate

No cosmetic SKU should be placed on the EU market merely because the website checkout works. Before sale, each cosmetic product needs the compliance work applicable under Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009, including:

- an EU Responsible Person;
- a cosmetic product safety assessment / Cosmetic Product Safety Report (CPSR);
- a Product Information File (PIF), kept for the required retention period;
- manufacture consistent with cosmetic Good Manufacturing Practice;
- CPNP notification before the product is placed on the EU market;
- compliant ingredient formulation and restrictions;
- final physical label information, including the Responsible Person details, nominal content where required, durability/PAO information where applicable, precautions, batch/reference identification, product function where not obvious, and INCI ingredient list;
- evidence supporting any explicit or implicit cosmetic performance claim.

The catalogue currently does not contain verified final INCI/label/CPSR/CPNP data. Do not invent these values in website copy. Add them only from the final verified product dossiers/labels.

## Cosmetic claims

Keep website claims cosmetic, clear and evidence-based. Avoid medical or therapeutic claims unless the product is lawfully classified and supported for that purpose. Ingredient properties must not be presented as proven finished-product effects without adequate evidence.

## Official reference points used for this checklist

- Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009 on cosmetic products, especially Articles 10, 11, 13, 19 and 20.
- Commission Regulation (EU) No 655/2013 on common criteria for cosmetic claims.
- EU Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU and Your Europe guidance on distance selling, withdrawal and personalised goods.

## Launch decision

Technical readiness is not the same as regulatory readiness. Body Glow can move to production only after the seller-information fields and the cosmetic launch gate above are confirmed with real documentation.
