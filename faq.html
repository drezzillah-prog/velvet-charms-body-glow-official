Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about made-to-order creations, personalization, production and delivery.

Are Velvet Charms products ready to ship?

No. Every creation is handmade after ordering. Payment reserves your place in our production schedule, and within 1–2 business days we confirm your production window and estimated dispatch date.

When does the displayed making time begin?

The approximate making time begins from your confirmed production start date, not automatically from the payment date. This allows us to give every order the care and attention it requires.

Can I request my order for a particular date?

Yes. Use the optional “Do you need it by a specific date?” field in your cart. We will always do our best to accommodate meaningful occasions, but the requested date is confirmed only after we review the creation and our current schedule.

Can products be personalized?

Yes. Available choices appear under Customize and may include fragrance, intensity, color, form, personal instructions and private reference photographs, depending on the product.

Will the displayed product price change?

No. The catalogue price is the product price for the item and listed customization choices. Delivery is treated separately according to your order value and destination.

How much does shipping cost?

Standard delivery within Romania is complimentary for orders of $100 or more. Shipping is added to orders below $100. International delivery is available worldwide and calculated separately according to destination, parcel size and weight.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery time is separate from production time and begins once your finished creation is dispatched. Your estimated dispatch date is confirmed first; delivery timing then depends on the destination and selected carrier.

How are payments processed?

Checkout is securely processed by PayPal. You may use your PayPal account or, where PayPal offers the option, an eligible debit or credit card without creating a PayPal account.

Are reference photographs private?

Yes. Reference photographs attached through the customization form are stored privately and used only to understand and complete your requested creation.

How can I ask a question before ordering?

Visit our Contact page and tell us what you have in mind. We usually reply within 1–2 business days.
