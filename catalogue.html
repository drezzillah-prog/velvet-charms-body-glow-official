Made Especially for You

Every Velvet Charms creation is individually handmade after your order is placed. Your payment reserves a place in our production schedule, and within 1–2 business days, you will receive confirmation of your production window and estimated dispatch date.

The approximate making time shown for each item begins from the confirmed production start date rather than the payment date. More intricate, fully personalized or photo-inspired creations will receive a carefully assessed individual timeframe before production begins.

Delivery time is calculated separately and begins once your finished creation is ready to be dispatched.
