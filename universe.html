The Velvet Universe

Velvet Charms brings handmade care, art and emotion together in creations that make everyday moments feel special. Each piece is designed not only to be enjoyed, but to carry a story, invite a ritual and—whenever possible—remain useful and beautiful long after its first purpose is complete.

How to read this page

Our products today—and the ideas we are building toward

The catalogue contains everything currently available to order. This page explains the larger Velvet Charms vision: how today’s handmade creations may grow into reusable collections, refills, collectible experiences and complete self-care rituals.

Available now

Handmade candles, body care, soaps, perfumes, textiles, gift sets and personalized creations are available today. The Second Life principle is already part of Velvet Charms: selected vessels and presentation pieces are designed to be kept, reused or repurposed instead of discarded.

In development

The next ideas being developed include refills, choosing your own vessel, hidden messages, scent stories, ritual cards, collectible charms, the Velvet Passport and build-your-own ritual boxes.

Long-term vision

The wider vision brings all future collections together: wellness gummies, spa textiles, massage tools, the Morning Ice Ritual, guided QR experiences, seasonal challenges and a digital Velvet community.

The long-term vision

What may join the universe next

These concepts are part of our creative roadmap, not products currently offered for sale. They will be introduced carefully, one collection at a time.

Velvet Box

Build a personal ritual box from care, candles, vessels, charms, messages and seasonal discoveries.

Velvet Spa

Bath salts, bath bombs, oils, towels, robes, spa headbands and complete at-home rituals.

Velvet Wellness

Future wellness concepts such as Beauty, Sleep, Energy, Focus, Balance and Immunity Charms, developed only with appropriate specialists and regulatory care.

Velvet Tools

Beautiful self-massage tools for face, scalp, hands, neck, back and feet in wood, glass, stone or stainless steel.

Morning Ice Ritual

A facial massage tool paired with its own silicone freezer mould. The perfectly fitted ice insert could carry the Velvet Charms mark and inspire a library of gentle ice-ritual recipes.

Rituals by QR

Short guided videos, playlists, tutorials and ritual cards designed to accompany selected creations.

Morning, Night & Spa Ritual Kits

Complete experiences rather than loose products: morning care with an ice roller and energising ritual; an evening box with candle, cream, tea and gentle guidance; or a spa set with scrub, body butter, oil, bath salts, gua sha and a QR playlist.

Functional Charms

A future family of carefully developed gummies: Sleep, Energy, Beauty, Focus, Balance, Happy and Immunity Charms—presented in elegant reusable vintage-inspired vessels and introduced only with specialist and regulatory support.

Ice Ritual Recipes

A library of ideas for the custom silicone mould: cucumber water, rose, green tea, chamomile or coffee inserts. The frozen piece would fit the massage tool precisely and carry the Velvet Charms mark.

The Digital Velvet Home

A place customers return to even when they are not shopping: a ritual journal, scent stories, QR guides, Passport tracking, Velvet Society levels, seasonal challenges, recipes and community surprises.

Collect the Symbols

Moon, star, cloud, butterfly, key, flower and heart symbols could be registered in a personal collection, unlocking a secret creation or experience when a set is completed.

Reusable Wellness Vessels

After the last gummy or ritual product, vintage-inspired containers could become homes for tea, spices, jewellery, sweets, dried flowers—or return to the refill cycle.
